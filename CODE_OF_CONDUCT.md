# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Foreman Solo is a small, friendly project for working developers. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith,
- give and accept constructive feedback,
- focus on the work, not the person,
- respect other people's time, including that of volunteer maintainers.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, trolling, publishing someone's private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any official project channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who do so repeatedly or seriously.

To report a problem, use the repository's private **Security** tab reporting form (choose "Report a vulnerability" and say it is a conduct report) until a dedicated contact is added here. Reports are handled confidentially.
